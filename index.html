About

I work at the intersection of intelligent computing, security and data-driven systems.

I am an Assistant Professor in Computer Science & Engineering. My academic background includes a PhD in Computer Science & Engineering from the University of Delhi, an M.E. in CSE from Thapar University, and a B.Tech in CSE from Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University.

Alongside research and teaching, I write across academic and creative forms, bringing together technology, observation and storytelling.
